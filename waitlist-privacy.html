A+ launch waitlist privacy notice

Effective October 17, 2026.

What we collect

If you register, Prime Bridge Sales Group Corp collects your email address, optional name, selected interest type and explicit opt-in acknowledgment. Do not submit wallet credentials, financial information, identifying documents or confidential records.

Purpose and delivery

We use this information to maintain an interest list and contact you about A+ platform launch announcements. This is separate from customer-service inquiries. We do not guarantee access, token allocation, financial returns, or a launch date.

Processors and storage

Your entry is sent to Web3Forms for processing and delivery to our business Gmail inbox. hCaptcha is used when you load spam protection. The front-end does not save subscription data to local storage or the private blockchain. Provider privacy policies: Web3Forms, hCaptcha, Google.

Unsubscribe and deletion

To stop launch announcements, email primebridgesalesgroup@gmail.com with subject UNSUBSCRIBE A+. We process unsubscribe requests manually; a request is not instantly automated. You may also request access, correction or deletion using the same contact. We must identify the appropriate record before changing it. The owner is responsible for maintaining a suppression list and honoring withdrawals before sending announcements.

Retention

Waitlist records remain until you unsubscribe, request deletion, or the interest list is closed, subject to necessary business or legal retention. Web3Forms provider copies may have separate retention settings. Do not assume removal from an email list instantly deletes third-party copies. Contact us for information about removal.
