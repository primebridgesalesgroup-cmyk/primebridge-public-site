CUSTOMER INQUIRY PRIVACY

Consultation & quote inquiry privacy notice

Effective October 16, 2026.

Who handles your inquiry

Prime Bridge Sales Group Corp handles consultation and quote inquiries for its business services. Contact primebridgesalesgroup@gmail.com with questions about your inquiry information.

Information and purpose

The inquiry form requests your name, reply email, service choice, request type, general town or region, and a brief project summary. We use these details to assess your request and reply about the service. Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing, book a consultation, establish a price, or accept a project engagement.

Do not submit financial details, account credentials, an exact street address, attachments, or confidential project records. Initial inquiries need only enough information for a service discussion.

Service providers

Sending shares the form details with Web3Forms for processing and delivery to our business Gmail inbox. Loading the spam check connects to Web3Forms and hCaptcha; those services may process technical request information to operate the form and prevent abuse. Their privacy notices describe their processing. Gmail also processes delivered messages under its provider terms.

Web3Forms privacy notice · hCaptcha privacy notice · Google privacy notice

Retention and deletion

Web3Forms is configured for its three-month submission retention period. Our retention target for inquiries that do not become customer projects is 90 days in the business inbox. Provider retention and Gmail deletion are separate: changing one does not delete the other. Project engagements require separate recordkeeping arrangements, which we will discuss when the scope is agreed.

Email primebridgesalesgroup@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of your inquiry information. Include only enough information to identify the inquiry; do not send identity documents or credentials. We will clarify the request with you before making changes to someone else's records.

On your device

The form does not save your entries in browser storage. A local draft remains on the page until you clear, reload, or leave it. Copying a draft places its text on your device clipboard. The spam check loads only when you select its load button. Your browser and service providers may handle technical information under their own settings and notices.
