## Daily intake

The business owner is the proposed responsible person until another named person
is assigned. Check Gmail Inbox and Spam each business day. Check Web3Forms quota
warnings and the dashboard; the free monthly limit is 250 submissions. Do not
promise a response time on the public website until staffing is confirmed.

Create labels `APlus-Inquiry-Open`, `APlus-Inquiry-Closed`, and
`APlus-Client-Records` if useful. Manually identify genuine inquiry emails using
the known test email and its subject, not an unverified sender guess. Label only
the messages concerned. Verify the entire conversation before changing labels,
because Gmail may show a whole thread for a message-level search match.

Check whether the inquiry needs a response, scope clarification, or a contractor
qualification check. Do not treat the form email as a booking, accepted scope,
payment, or signed engagement. Ask for sensitive documents only through a
separately approved process. Do not forward inquiry details without a business
need and appropriate authorization.

## 90-day inbox target

At intake, record the original inquiry date and a review date before day 83.
Review at least weekly. This gives time to remove closed, unconverted inquiries
from the business mailbox by the owner's 90-day target. A reply can change a
thread's displayed date, so date searches are a review aid, not proof of age.

For candidate review only, Gmail supports a search such as:

`label:APlus-Inquiry-Closed older_than:80d`

Inspect every result against the original inquiry date and whether it became a
customer project. Do not bulk-delete from a search result or assume a label
excludes other messages in the same thread. Separately review unlabelled inquiry
notifications so omissions cannot evade retention review.

For closed, unconverted inquiries, the owner should review the exact messages,
move only those messages to Trash, then review those specific Trash items and
permanently delete them by the target date. **Moving to Trash alone is not
permanent deletion**: Gmail normally keeps Trash for up to 30 additional days.
Do not empty unrelated Trash or delete customer-project records under this rule.
Permanent deletion is an explicit owner action; this guide authorizes no agent
to perform it automatically.

For project records that need different handling, agree the recordkeeping scope
at engagement and document it separately. Keep a minimal review log with dates,
counts and completion status; do not reproduce the customer's message or email
address in a separate deletion log. Review local downloads, forwarded copies,
and replies containing quoted inquiry text as part of the same process.

## Requests about inquiry information

Identify the relevant inquiry with the least necessary information. Do not
request passwords or identity documents. Confirm the requester is entitled to
the relevant records before sharing or changing them. Review Web3Forms and Gmail
separately; provider retention does not remove delivered inbox copies.
