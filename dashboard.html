Security & privacy

Connection boundaries
  • Your wallet stays in your wallet.Keys and recovery phrases are never requested. This page only uses wallet account and read methods.
  • No automatic wallet connection.You choose the provider and explicitly connect. Your wallet provider may contact its configured RPC when you request a read.
  • Wallet data stays in page memory.Addresses and balances are not written to browser storage or sent to Prime Bridge by this page. Reloading clears them. Exporting creates a CSV on your device.
  • Private records need authenticated access.A connected address is not an authenticated customer account or proof of legal ownership. Private ledger, license and inventory records remain unavailable until connected securely.
  • Separate One-World accounts.One-World sign-in uses its own application and account session. It does not sign you in to this workspace.
